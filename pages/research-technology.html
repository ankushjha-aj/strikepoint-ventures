Research

From a hypothesis to a strategy you can stand behind.

01

Signal & Hypothesis Discovery

We search for repeatable, tradable patterns that have a clear economic reason to exist. A candidate signal with no rationale other than a good backtest does not go live.

02

Strategy Building

We take a loose idea to a fully specified rule set: entries, exits, sizing, risk limits and regime filters, written precisely enough to automate with no interpretation.

03

Validation & Resilience Testing

Walk forward testing, out of sample checks, Monte Carlo resampling, parameter sweeps and regime analysis. If your Sharpe holds up on only one parameter set, we say so before the market does.

OUR STRONGEST SPECIALTY

Options & Volatility Studies

Volatility surfaces and Greeks behaviour. Signals based on IV percentile, volatility of volatility, model free implied volatility, realised volatility and the variance risk premium, covering index options and more.

05

Execution-Conscious Modelling

Backtests that reflect reality: transaction costs, slippage, fill probability, margin and liquidity limits. A strategy that works only with free, instant fills does not really work.

06

Strategy Review & Independent Opinion

Already bought or built a strategy? We examine it for overfitting, look ahead bias, survivorship bias and blind spots in execution. Independent, direct and written down.

How a project runs

No open ended billing. No unpleasant surprises.

  1. Scoping callFREE

    Thirty minutes. You explain what you want to achieve and we raise the awkward questions early. We say upfront whether it can be done.

  2. Written proposal

    Fixed scope, timeline, milestones and cost, in writing, before any work starts.

  3. Research & backtesting

    Hypothesis testing on quality data using execution aware assumptions. You see interim results, not a black box.

  4. Validation gauntlet

    Walk forward, out of sample, sensitivity and regime tests. Most ideas fail here. That is the purpose.

  5. Build, deploy & handover

    Working software at each stage, then deployed on your infrastructure with the report, code, backtest framework, documentation, tests and a support period. All of it is yours, under NDA.

Our standard

Most ideas do not pass our validation. Good.

Most of the strategy ideas we study do not make it through the validation gauntlet. We treat that as a success, because the cheapest place to lose money is in a backtest.

We will never inflate a Sharpe ratio, cherry pick a date range or conceal a drawdown to make a report look better. You pay for the truth about your strategy, not for flattery.
