How we think

A worked example: volatility of volatility

Take one signal from our volatility toolkit. Volatility of volatility measures how sharply the volatility surface bends near the money, which is a direct read on the convexity the market is pricing.

Tracked against its own history with Z scores and quantiles, that curvature becomes a tradable signal. When it stretches past statistically derived thresholds, it points to option structures such as butterflies or iron condors positioned to capture the mispricing.

Every idea we research gets the same treatment: a clear economic rationale, a statistical definition, and a rule for when to act. Share yours and we will walk you through the methodology applied to it.

Our standard

Most ideas fail our validation. Good.
