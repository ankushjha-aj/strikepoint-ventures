Financial Markets Software

Custom software for financial markets: execution systems, backtesting engines, analytics terminals, risk tools and market data infrastructure, for any asset class, broker or exchange API. Built by a team that runs its own automated capital on the same systems.

Book a free scoping call → Thirty minutes, no commitment. If we are the wrong fit we will say so.

Most financial markets software fails in the gap between the model and the live market. We build systems that close that gap: realistic simulation, robust execution, defensive risk controls and infrastructure that keeps running when markets get violent.

We work with prop desks, brokers, independent systematic traders, fintech teams and research firms worldwide. If you want it, we can build and run the whole stack.

How we help

Everything your trading operation needs, from data to execution.

Automated Execution Systems

Signal handling, order routing for complex option structures, position management, real time risk checks, kill switches and complete audit trails. Works across brokers and accounts, deployed on your infrastructure.

Backtesting & Simulation

Event driven backtesters with realistic fills, slippage, costs and margin modelling. Built for options: Greeks, IV surfaces, expiry mechanics and gamma exposure handled correctly.

Market Data Pipelines

Real time and historical ingestion, tick storage on TimescaleDB, data quality checks, corporate action adjustment and clean APIs for your research stack.

Dashboards & Terminals

Live P&L and exposure monitoring plus volatility and positioning analytics. Web based, on your servers or in the cloud, built on the same framework as our own RTX terminal.

Broker & Exchange Integrations

Adapters for any venue with an API: Indian brokers such as Kite, Shoonya and XTS, global gateways like Interactive Brokers, and crypto exchanges through Binance and CCXT.

Strategy Audit & System Rescue

Inherited a bot that double fires orders? A backtest that looks too good? We audit existing systems for execution bugs, overfitting and infrastructure risk, then fix them.
